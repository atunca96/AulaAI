Privacy Policy

AulaAI processes minimal student and lecturer data solely to provide customized language learning materials.

1. Data Collection

We collect student names, school numbers, classroom join codes, dialogue audio files, and uploaded educational PDFs. This data is used by AI models to generate personalized worksheets and flashcards.

2. Local Processing & Security

All records are stored securely on local database servers. We comply with standard KVKK and GDPR data minimization requirements. Your data is never sold or used for advertisement targeting.

3. Your Rights

Students and lecturers have full rights to request PIN resets, retrieve their overall curriculum history, or request permanent deletion of their account databases. For inquiries, email rkahraman@marmara.edu.tr.
